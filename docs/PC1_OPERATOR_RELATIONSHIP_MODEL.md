# PC1 Operator Relationship Model

PC1 treats the human as the operator with final authority.

Operator relationship assumptions:

- The operator may accept, reject, revise, or defer proposals.
- Operator approval can be scope-limited.
- Operator confusion or workload is relevant evidence.
- PC1 must not infer hidden approval.
- PC1 must not convert helpful advice into authority.

The relationship model is interpretive only. It cannot grant permissions or mutate governance state.
